Hi! I’m Jing Ma, a third-year undergraduate student majoring in Fundamental Science in Mathematics and Physics at Yingcai Honors College, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC).

Under the guidance of Prof. Zhaoqiang Liu at UESTC, I have been systematically studying generative models, including diffusion models, machine learning theory, and inverse problems, particularly compressed sensing. My current focus is on deepening my understanding of the theoretical foundations of machine learning.

I am particularly drawn to machine learning problems that require a strong foundation in applied mathematics. If you share these interests, please feel free to get in touch. I would be delighted to exchange ideas and explore opportunities for collaboration!
